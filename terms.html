The basics

These terms cover using this site and buying from Panda Sports Memorabilia (“Panda,” “we,” “us”). By browsing the site or placing an order, you're agreeing to them. If something here doesn't sit right with you, email us before you order — we'd rather sort it out than have you feel stuck with it.

Who we are

Panda is a family-run shop selling authenticated sports memorabilia — signed jerseys, balls, game-used gear and photography. We buy every item ourselves and resell it; nothing here is sold at auction or on consignment. We also donate 10% of sales proceeds to a cancer charity or foundation of our choice. You can read more about how we operate on our About page.

Buying from us

Listings

We describe every item as accurately as we can, including what documentation — if any — it carries. A listing's photographs are of the actual piece, not a stock image, unless it says otherwise; actual colour can still vary slightly from what you see, depending on your screen. Condition notes are our own assessment unless a piece carries a third-party grading slab — ask us for more photos or specifics before you buy if you want a closer look.

Prices

Prices are shown in USD. Each listed price is all-inclusive — it covers standard (priority) shipping through FedEx, UPS or another reputable carrier, any applicable taxes, fees and insurance, so nothing is added afterward. The one exception: if you specifically request faster, expedited shipping, that costs extra on top of the listed price. There's no bidding: every item is sold at a fixed price. If we catch a pricing error before your order ships, we'll contact you rather than silently honour or cancel it.

Payment

Reserving an item saves a card on file with Stripe, our payment processor — entered directly into their own secure field, never seen or stored by us. Nothing is charged at that point. Once your order ships, that saved card is charged automatically for the listed amount, without further action from you.

One-of-a-kind items

Most of what we sell is a single physical piece, not stock we can reorder. Very occasionally, two orders can land for the same item before our system catches up — if that happens to you, we simply won't invoice you for it and will tell you why, rather than substitute something you didn't ask for.

Order acceptance

Placing an order is you asking to buy something, not a guarantee we can fill it. We'll confirm by email once it's accepted; until then, either of us can cancel it.

Shipping & sales policy

Shipping timelines and international availability are covered on our FAQ; shipping cost itself is already part of the listed price. Our sales policy — every sale is final, no refunds or exchanges — is covered in full on our Refund Policy page. Rather than duplicate either here, those pages are the current word on both — check them before you order.

Risk of loss passes to you once an order is handed to the carrier. A lost or damaged parcel doesn't change the sale — it's final either way, the same as any other order.
