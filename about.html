About us

Collecting shouldn't be out of reach

Panda Sports Memorabilia sells authentic sports memorabilia at prices ordinary fans can actually pay. A family-run shop with a deliberately short list of promises. This page is about how we work, which is the part you can judge us on from the first order.

Why we started

Find cool stuff. Price it reasonably.

We're a family owned business. Our mission is simple: find cool stuff and resell it at a reasonable price.

Why the name Panda? Simple. Watch some TikTok videos of pandas and you'll agree — they're some of the coolest animals out there.

We'd rather earn trust with specifics than borrow it with testimonials. Here's what each piece is, what documentation it carries, and what it costs — nothing added at checkout, true on order one or order one thousand.

Stock comes through an established sports memorabilia auction house. What we won't do is write our own certificates — whatever documentation a piece carries is exactly what the listing will tell you, no more, no less.

What we will and won't list

  • Items we have in hand before they go up
  • Pieces whose documentation we can describe honestly
  • One all-inclusive price, with nothing added later
  • Anything we'd have to overstate to sell
  • Certificates issued by whoever is selling the piece
  • Replicas or reprints described as anything else

What we promise

Five things, in plain terms

Short list, deliberately. We'd rather commit to a few things and keep them.

  • We've held everything we sell

    Every piece passes through our hands before it ships — checked against its paperwork here, never drop-shipped from someone else's photo.

  • We check before we say it's authentic

    Every piece is checked against its documentation here, in hand, before it's ever listed — not something we say and hope is true.

  • The price is the price

    We buy every piece ourselves and sell it at one all-inclusive price — no auction, no bidding, nothing added later. What's on the listing, standard shipping and all, is exactly what you pay — unless you ask for faster, expedited shipping, which costs extra.

  • We tell you what we actually know

    Listings say exactly what documentation a piece carries and where it came from. If we don't know something, we say so.

  • 5% of sales proceeds go to fighting cancer

    Not a promotion and not a rotating cause — a fixed share of every order, on every item, every day we're open, to a cancer charity or foundation of our choice. Cancer has touched our family, like it has most families, and this is how we give back.
