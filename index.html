Why Panda

Three things we intend to get right

We find cool stuff and sell it at a fair price.

  • Say what a piece actually is

    Every listing states exactly what documentation a piece carries, even when the answer is “none.” We never write our own certificates — only real, third-party paperwork counts.

  • Show you the whole picture

    Condition, wear, what's included and what isn't — stated plainly. If a piece has a flaw, you'll know before you buy it.

  • Price it for fans

    One all-inclusive price, shown up front before you ever reserve it — no buyer's premium, no auction fees, nothing added later. Your card isn't charged until your order ships, and we get it moving fast once it does.

How it will work

Three steps, every item

One fixed price, every time — no bidding, no auction house in the middle. Three simple steps.

  1. 1

    Sourced

    Stock comes through an established sports memorabilia auction house.

  2. 2

    Checked

    Every item arrives with us first. We check it against its documentation and confirm the condition before it's ever listed.

  3. 3

    Listed

    Real photographs, a plain statement of what the piece carries, and one price with nothing added at checkout.
